## Distinct `type` values and interpretation

| type | rows | conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| `national` | 3 | RECRUTEMENT NATIONAL pools (médecine, pharmacie, chirurgie dentaire); `wilaya` is `null`. → scope **national** |
| `grande_ecole` | 24 | Écoles supérieures / grandes écoles, national recruitment. → scope **national** |
| `university` | 4,418 | Ordinary university filières, access governed by geographic circles. → scope **regional** |

**Caveat found during inspection:** `type` under-tags the grandes écoles. 37 rows have a
filière label prefixed `FB ` (formation de base of an école supérieure, e.g.
`FB INF ECOLE SUPERIEURE ... BEJAIA` = ESTIN) but only some carry `type=grande_ecole`;
the rest are typed `university` even though they recruit nationally. The builder therefore
counts a row as national recruitment when `type ∈ {national, grande_ecole}` **or** its
filière starts with `FB `. Écoles normales supérieures (873 rows) are **not** treated as
national: their filières are per-wilaya quotas (`... pour bacheliers X`) → regional.

Entry-level scope rule: no rows → `unknown`; all rows national-recruitment → `national`;
rows exist but none carries a wilaya (null-wilaya school rows, e.g. `enscrbc`) → `national`;
otherwise → `regional`. Mixed national+university entries are `regional` (circle-based
university access is the default path; the school rows still appear in `establishments`).

Other conventions:
- `offeredIn` excludes rows with `wilaya = null` (the "National" pseudo-wilaya); such rows
  are recorded via `nationalRow: true` on the entry.
- `establishments` deduped, capped at **5 per wilaya**.
- `notOfferedNote_ar` is generic per scope — **no circle-based redirection targets are
  invented** (official circles are handled separately).
